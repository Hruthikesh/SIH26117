# Refinery glossary (excerpt)

- **P&ID** — Piping and Instrumentation Diagram.
- **PSV** — Pressure Safety Valve (relief device).
- **CSO / CSC** — Car-Sealed Open / Closed (a valve secured in position).
- **MTBF** — Mean Time Between Failures.
- **HAZOP** — Hazard and Operability study.
- **MoC** — Management of Change.
- **SIF / SIS** — Safety Instrumented Function / System.
- **Flush plan** — API 682 seal flush arrangement (e.g. plan 11, 32, 52).
- **Spec break** — a change of piping specification along a line.
