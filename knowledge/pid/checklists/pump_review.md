# Pump review checklist (P&ID)

- Discharge check valve present (prevents reverse flow).
- Positive-displacement pump: relief device on discharge (dead-head protection).
- Suction and discharge block valves for isolation.
- Minimum-flow / recirculation provision where required by service.
- Seal flush plan piping shown and consistent with the datasheet (e.g. plan 11/32/52).
- Standby pump and auto-start where the service demands it.
